# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone participating in this project — in issues, discussions, pull requests
and reviews — is expected to make participation harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of who they are.

## Our standards

**Do:** be direct and technical; critique code, not people; assume good faith;
accept that "no" to a feature is not an insult; credit others' work.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; publish others' private
information; derail threads; submit AI-generated spam issues or PRs.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to all project spaces. Report unacceptable behavior privately to
the maintainer via GitHub. Consequences scale from a warning to a permanent
ban at the maintainer's discretion.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
